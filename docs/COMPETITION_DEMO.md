# Competition Demo Script

This is the safest three-minute path for judges. It runs without authentication,
uses simulated family data, and does not upload photos.

## Before entering the room

- Use the latest successful `sila-web-release` artifact or a locally verified
  release build.
- Use a landscape laptop or tablet at 100% browser zoom.
- Confirm the welcome screen and **Try the 3-minute Competition Demo** button
  appear.
- Complete one rehearsal from beginning to Impact.
- Keep this script open on a second device, not over the demo window.

## Three-minute narration

### 0:00–0:25 — The problem and promise

Say:

> Families can be in the same room but still feel disconnected. Sila turns a
> few shared minutes into a repeatable connection habit: do something together,
> celebrate fairly, choose a reward, and preserve the memory.

Point out the bilingual Sila identity and UAE Year of Family 2026 treatment.
Select **Try the 3-minute Competition Demo**.

### 0:25–0:55 — Mission

Explain that the demo family is private simulated data. Complete the mission
verification.

Say:

> The first step is participation, not screen time for its own sake. A shared
> mission creates the real-world moment; the app records the outcome.

### 0:55–1:30 — Competition

Choose **Sara** for the final family quiz answer and crown the winner.

Say:

> Friendly competition gives every family member a reason to join. Official
> daily, weekly, and monthly formats extend this into a fair family rhythm.

Pause briefly on the champion animation and token award.

### 1:30–2:05 — Reward

Select **Choose a family reward**, then redeem **Choose Family Dinner**.

Say:

> Progress becomes a shared offline experience. Tokens are not cash and the
> reward is not an advertisement—it is a family agreement.

### 2:05–2:35 — Memory

Select **Save this night**, then **Save family memory**.

Say:

> The experience does not disappear when the game ends. The family keeps a
> story it created together.

### 2:35–3:00 — Impact

Let the Impact screen show the completed mission, champion, redeemed reward,
saved memory, and remaining balance.

Close with:

> Sila is one connected loop, not a collection of isolated features. It is
> bilingual, family-scoped, release-tested, and designed around the UAE’s Year
> of Family: stronger roots, closer bonds, and shared growth.

## If a judge asks to explore

- **Games:** show Family Impostor category choice, the shared 1/3/5-round setup,
  Trivia or Emoji Guess, and Caption Battle prompt variety.
- **Competition:** show Daily Challenge, Weekly Championship, and Monthly Cup.
- **Personalization:** open Settings and switch among Sila Light, Dark, and UAE
  Family Year themes; switch to Arabic to demonstrate RTL.
- **Privacy:** explain that production AI routes require Firebase authentication,
  proof photos are not retained by the mission record, and rules are emulator
  tested.
- **Evidence:** show the latest green pull-request checks and downloadable web
  artifact rather than making an unsupported reliability claim.

## Fallback plan

If network, Firebase, or the AI gateway is unavailable, stay in the Competition
Demo. It is intentionally self-contained. Refresh once only if the page fails to
paint; otherwise move to a prepared screenshot or screen recording and continue
the same narration. Do not attempt live credential or production configuration
changes in front of judges.

## Claims discipline

Say “designed to,” “tested to,” or “our pilot target is” until real pilot data
exists. Do not claim measured family wellbeing improvement, production scale,
or regulatory certification without supporting evidence.
